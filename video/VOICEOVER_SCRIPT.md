# Plume: voiceover script

Target length about 2:15 (roughly 350 words at an ad pace of about 150 words a minute).
Read it out, record it as one voice memo, and I will line the motion graphics up with your transcript.

## How to record

- Quiet room, soft furnishing around you (a closet full of clothes works well). Phone 20 to 30 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side so you don't pop on the "p"s.
- Record the whole script in one take. Leave about **one second of silence between blocks**.
- Messed up a line? Pause, then read the **whole sentence** again. I keep the last take.
- Tone: warm, confident, unhurried. Think Apple product film, not radio ad. Smile on the upbeat lines.
- `…` means a beat (half a second). **Bold** words get the emphasis. `//` means a slightly longer pause.
- Send me the file (the `.m4a` from Voice Memos is fine). Drop it in `video/assets/` or give me the path.

## [01] The boring bank
*On screen: an ordinary KBC Mobile home screen. Balance, a long list of card payments scrolling past.*

> This is your banking app.
> It knows every coffee. Every grocery run. Every late-night order.
> And it tells you exactly what you spent… // after you've spent it.

## [02] The opt-in
*On screen: a "New in KBC Mobile" card appears. Tap. The opt-in screen: challenge, fund, consent, "Hatch my swan".*

> What if your bank could help you **before**?
> Meet **Plume**. A new tab, right inside KBC Mobile.
> Choose your challenge. Choose the fund where your savings will grow.
> One tap to opt in… // and hatch your swan.

## [03] One number a day
*On screen: the egg hatches, the pond world bursts out of the phone. The big number: "Left to spend today".*

> From now on, you get **one** number a day.
> What's left to spend. // Today.
> No spreadsheets. No lectures.
> A daily limit, learned from your **own** transactions.

## [04] The swan
*On screen: the swan moves through every state. Sun, clouds, rain, storm. The health bar drops.*

> Stay under it, and your swan thrives.
> Keep overspending, and the weather turns.
> Your swan gets tired… then sick… // and if you let it go, it dies.
> One bad day hurts a little. A bad **habit** hurts a lot.

## [05] The buffer
*On screen: unspent money drips into the buffer. A 899 euro laptop hits; the buffer absorbs part, the limit dips, then recovers.*

> Every euro you don't spend lands in your **buffer**.
> So when life happens, a new laptop, a bike repair, your buffer catches it. Not today's limit.
> Can't cover it all? Your limit dips a little, until you've earned it back.

## [06] Payday
*On screen: payday sweep. The buffer flows into the KBC fund, the lake grows, the passive income counter ticks up.*

> And on payday, your buffer is invested in your KBC fund.
> Your lake grows.
> Good habits… // become passive income.

## [07] Under the hood
*On screen: the model pipeline. Transactions sorted into income, fixed costs, subscriptions, everyday spending and one-offs. Noise filter, baseline, weekday rhythm, target, limit.*

> Under the hood? // No black box.
> The model reads ninety days of history.
> It sets aside rent, bills and subscriptions.
> It flags big one-offs as **noise**, because one expensive purchase doesn't change who you are.
> It learns your baseline, and your weekly rhythm: more room on Saturdays, if that's your night out.
> Then it asks you to save five, ten or twenty percent. Never more than your income allows.
> And it **freezes** that profile, so saving is never punished with a tighter limit.

## [08] Explainable, and one API
*On screen: the "Why this limit today?" breakdown. Then the architecture: KBC app, Black Swan API, the calls between them.*

> Every limit explains itself, line by line.
> For KBC, it's one API. Push transactions in, pull the swan back out.
> Opt-in only. Deterministic. And opting out deletes **everything**.

## [09] Four people, four swans
*On screen: four phones side by side, four customers, four very different ponds.*

> Sofie saves every payday.
> Lucas is a freelancer with no fixed salary. It still works.
> Emma's routine costs more than she earns.
> Jonas splurges the week he gets paid.
> Four people. // Four swans.

## [10] Outro
*On screen: the swan glides in, logo lockup, "Plume for KBC Mobile", credit to team Black Swans.*

> Plume. For KBC.
> Spend with intent. // Keep your swan alive.
