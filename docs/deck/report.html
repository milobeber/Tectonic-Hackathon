KBC case · Tectonic Hackathon · Team Black Swans

Four customers, two paydays

Two good spenders and two bad ones, simulated with our real model and game engine on synthetic KBC-style transactions. For each: what the model learned before they opted in, the daily goal it set, what their app showed on key days, and how their swan fared.

Learns your normal

90 days before opt-in: wage, fixed costs, subscriptions and day-to-day habits. Big one-offs are left out as noise.

Sets a daily goal

Habits minus 10%, never more than income allows after fixed costs, adjusted per weekday.

Runs payday to payday

A cycle starts when the wage lands (the biggest income that repeats monthly). No wage: calendar months.

Buffers and invests

Unspent limit fills a buffer that is invested at payday. Big buys come out of it first; when it runs dry, limits drop.

Keeps a swan alive

Health follows the 7-day pace against the limits. Buys you cannot afford hit hard. A dead swan hatches again next cycle.

Synthetic data from backend/blackswan/cases.py, simulated with backend/blackswan/engine.py. Regenerate with scripts/export_deck_cases.py. Fund returns are illustrative, not investment advice.
